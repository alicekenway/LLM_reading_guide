Performance of Image →Text
To comprehensively evaluate the capabilities on Image →Text, we compare Qwen2.5-Omni with the
recent state-of-the-art large vision language model Qwen2.5-VL-7B and other best-performing omni
models. As illustrated in Table 5, Qwen2.5-Omni demonstrates comparable performance to Qwen2.5-
VL-7B, and attains better results on MMMU, MathVision, MMBench-V1.1-EN, TextVQA, DocVQA and
ChartQA than any other open-sourced omni models. Additionally, Qwen2.5-Omni also surpasses GPT-
4o-mini on most benchmarks. These results reveal the excellent capability of our model on image
understanding.
